How training travels

01

Build

Create a session with purpose. Blocks, durations and instructions that make your method easy to follow.

02

Share

One link carries the whole session. Whoever opens it gets exactly what you built, with your name on it.

03

Climb

Open it and train. No account needed. Your private training history is yours; publish or sync only when you choose.

04

Remix

Adapt it to you. Fork it, change it, make it yours. Credit stays with the original.

Train on purpose

You know your body.
You make the calls.

Every session ends with one question: how did it feel? That answer, not a completion percentage, steers what comes next. Skipping a block is a training decision, not a failure.

  • Green. Strong and in control. Next session can take a little more.
  • Orange. Right at the edge. Hold the volume, recover well.
  • Red. Too much today. Back off smart, come back stronger.

For coaches and gyms

Turn your coaching into a library

  • Build once, share forever. Your sessions as links your athletes open on their own phone. No account needed on their side.
  • Your method, organised. Warm-ups, protocols and progressions in one consistent, trainable format.
  • Private first. Share with your group by link today. Publishing to the open library coming later this year, always your choice.
